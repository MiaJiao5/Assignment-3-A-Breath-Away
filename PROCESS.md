# Development process

## Product intent

A Breath Away offers a few calm minutes of wandering, listening, and leaving a small thought behind. The English-first bilingual interface is part of the core experience. The Simplified Chinese copy keeps the same gentle, slightly playful tone and uses concise labels for mobile screens.

## Implementation

`BilingualText` overlays two hidden, accessibility-excluded language reserves with one visible translation in a CSS grid. The larger wrapped height and intrinsic width determine a shared footprint. Body blocks align their first lines at the top; labels remain centered within buttons. Typography stays shared across languages, while questionnaire rows, modal content, sky quotes, feedback and control labels retain their geometry. The input action, leave action and rest action have stable dimensions. No scene, character or audio component is keyed by language. The bilingual layout regression compares pixel bounds and font metrics at 320x568, 320x667, 390x844, 414x844 and desktop sizes across the cover, headphones, all questions, guides, roaming, seating, message form, farewell and portal panel.

The cover uses locally bundled Allura Regular at its real 400 weight. Its thin connectors, open capitals and flowing terminals are laid out as A / Breath / Away within a single SVG. One shared user-space paint field flows through the whole composition: a lavender-to-blue-to-mint gradient and an elliptical pink/peach/champagne overlay positioned behind the middle-right letters. The background uses diffuse low-opacity color fields on warm-white paper, with a reduced yellow glow. A Breath Away remains English in both interface languages. The button reads Begin Your Little Escape / 开启短暂漫游; its existing appearance and bottom placement are retained.

The farewell layout follows a quiet illustrated composition: a small closing line, one larger waving SVG spirit with a subtly textured cloud edge, a serif reassurance and a short body-font caption. The text card is removed. One glass primary action and a secondary text link retain the original restart/travel behavior. Decorative nebula layers, star glints and the companion's soft aura inherit the current planet's colors through CSS variables; no planet background mapping is changed. The responsive grid scales the portrait down on short displays, retains bilingual footprints, and respects reduced motion.

The existing React scene flow, Three.js planet, spirit animations, mood mapping, and Web Audio synthesis remain in place. A typed Language union (en / zh), a central translation dictionary, and a shared React context supply all display copy. This includes questions, options, planet descriptions, controls, tooltips, placeholders, accessibility labels, built-in floating quotes, and feedback.

The enlarged 3D companion no longer uses an expanded back-face outline. A low-amplitude deformed sphere, shared physical material with velvet sheen, subtle bump relief and pastel environmental tint provide volume. A transparent radial sprite feathers around the silhouette, retaining the existing contact shadow and tiny feet. Seated breathing has a 5.6-second period, millimeter-scale bobbing and a small listening lean; reduced motion freezes these decorative changes. Camera framing retains its local push-in at approximately 1.3 times the walking silhouette. `PlanetAtmosphere` adds only decorative, pointer-transparent CSS layers: slower far mist, nearer haze, fourteen soft dust points, four slow twinkles and two long-cycle sky meteors. It does not remount the WebGL scene or affect movement, input, audio or translations. Mobile framing, no-outline rendering, breathing limits, reduced motion, music continuity and texture disposal are covered by the spirit/atmosphere and seated-framing regressions.

The language provider owns only the language preference. It never replaces the application or keys the scene by language. Switching updates rendered text without changing the scene, movement references, selected planet, input, audio configuration, or music timers. The document language and title also update. UI messages store semantic keys so an already-visible error or notification can change language; user text remains separate and is never translated or trimmed for display.

The language pill occupies a reserved row above the header, with selected-state semantics and keyboard focus styling. Header actions wrap below it. Panels leave the switch accessible. Long labels wrap, inputs can shrink, and shorter screens allow vertical scrolling. Questionnaire answers advance immediately on selection. Its footer contains only the centered Back / 返回 action, preserving answers and returning from the first question to the interlude. The skip action and handler are removed; browser fixtures now complete three real choices to reach their starting planet. The transition timer is independent of language and cancels if the visitor leaves during the transition.

localStorage access is guarded. The browser locale is never consulted when selecting the initial language. A valid saved Chinese preference restores Chinese; otherwise the initial language is English. A storage failure does not prevent switching during the visit.

## Surface movement and guided resting

The original movement kept the companion at a fixed world position and rotated the planet. Character movement now combines walking over the surface with a gentle counter-rotation of the globe. Integrated joystick travel updates the companion?s transported tangent frame, which aligns its body and contact shadow with the local surface normal. Heading is relative to this frame, so vertical input no longer produces mismatched feet. Each surface step applies 45% of its inverse rotation to the shared planet parent. This gives rolling in the direction opposite the walk without pinning the character in place. Camera offsets also use the transformed world-space normal, preserving the rear resting view. Rotation freezes on sitting. The previous longitude/latitude limits are removed. Movement now transports a quaternion tangent frame over the sphere, so pole crossings and complete circuits remain continuous. The camera follows that frame while keeping the resting view, foot contact, and world-space shadow aligned. The old duplicate step and swipe movement loops have been removed.

The pre-rest movement panel contains only the joystick. Its translated instruction appears at the top of the scene in place of the atmosphere subtitle. The keyboard remains available. On each planet, a thin, translucent cream-gold curve and a soft arch guide the initial walk to the Listening Stone. The Sit & Listen action appears in a reserved slot above the joystick when the character is nearby, and becomes Stand Up after sitting. Tapping the companion or stone does not sit automatically. The arrival threshold is 0.38 radians and the departure threshold is 0.46 radians, preventing flicker near the boundary. The reserved slot avoids scene movement when the button appears or disappears. Direction, landmark, and collapse buttons remain absent.

Rest cancels held input and spring velocity. The render loop and landmark interaction handlers enforce this lock. The companion turns away from the audience, and the camera moves behind it using the same local surface frame. The complete seated framing described below keeps the globe visible. Stand Up restores the walking view. Reduced-motion preferences use the destination view immediately. Language changes neither restart the camera nor affect audio timers.

The joystick hides during rest. The message form appears only while seated, and its submit handler also checks the resting state. Drafts persist when the form hides. Language remains available. The Sound On / mute toggle is removed, while sitting continues to start the music. The farewell action unlocks after the first Stand Up and remains available while roaming. A bilingual one-time guide points to its fixed top-left location.

The updated rest-browser.cjs checks actual keyboard and pointer movement, opposing globe rotation in all four directions, frozen rotation during rest, the root's radius and alignment with the surface normal, contact-shadow distance, initial guidance, contextual stone interaction, a rear camera angle, blocked scene input while resting, resume, drafts, languages, and mobile layouts. Before/after screenshots are inspected. The bilingual regression covers the full application flow and audio continuity using the updated sitting interaction.

## Questionnaire sound bridge

The first user gesture unlocks Web Audio and fades in an extremely quiet procedural bed: a low A-major-seventh sine stack, filtered pink noise, and a gain LFO whose complete breath lasts six seconds. Answer buttons trigger short two-note water-ripple overtones with small pitch variations. The final answer or skip action briefly raises the bed while the transition shimmer gains a filtered-noise whoosh. The bed then remains at a lower planet level instead of stopping. Sitting ducks it to a background level and fades a separate planet-music gain bus in over 2.8 seconds; standing fades the music bus out over 2.2 seconds and restores the environmental bed.

The four owner-supplied recordings are copied without transcoding into `public/audio/` and selected by `PLANET_AUDIO_URLS`. A shared hidden player routes through a cached MediaElementAudioSourceNode and an independent music GainNode. Smoothstep gain curves start and finish softly: successful playback fades in for 2.8 seconds, standing fades out to zero for 2.2 seconds and pauses playback, and farewell fades for 1.4 seconds. Rescheduling holds the current gain and cancels old automation. Playback session identifiers cancel obsolete promises and delayed cleanup, so a rapid re-sit cannot be paused by the previous fade. Procedural melodies remain available when a URL is cleared, but do not overlap configured MP3 tracks.
